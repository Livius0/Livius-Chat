What you can do

Groups & chat

Text and voice channels, replies, mentions, reactions and file sharing.

Voice

Noise suppression, push-to-talk and per-user volume.

Screen sharing

Up to 1080p at 60 fps, with app audio. Webcam with blurred background.

Soundboard

Everyone adds their own sounds and everyone hears them.

3D plaza & games

A shared 3D plaza with Livius City, an arcade, a casino and more.

Friends & calls

Add friends with a code, private chats and calls that ring like a phone call.

All your devices

PC and phone on the same account, linked with a code and kept in sync.

Your language

English, Italian and Spanish. Follows your system language.

What's new

  • 3D plaza: new camera, benches, dogs to pet, a bar and jackets in the shop.
  • Livius City: bigger board with stations and utilities, endless games and a list of your properties.
  • Casino and arcade: roulette in sync for everyone and new ways to play Evil and Shot.
  • Streaming: stream audio only for viewers, and share the audio of a single app.
  • More platforms: Linux and a web version that runs in the browser.
